Body Oracle — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Reality Design Lab

Body Oracle is an artwork app that turns body poses into characters inspired by Chinese oracle-bone inscriptions. It is made by Reality Design Lab and published on the App Store by Holo Interactive US, Inc. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Summary

Body Oracle does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

Camera

The app uses the camera to recognise body poses. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device by an on-device model and are discarded straight away. They are never saved by the app and never sent off the device.

Microphone and Photos

If you choose to record a video with the Record button, the app records the screen and the microphone and saves the video to your Photos library. The microphone is used only while you are recording, and access to Photos is limited to adding your recordings. Recordings stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
